Admission Process

  1. Fill out the online application form on our portal.
  2. Submit copies of your academic transcripts and identity proof.
  3. Appear for the College Entrance Examination (CEE).
  4. Attend the personal interview round if shortlisted.
  5. Verify your original documents and secure your seat by paying the admission fee.
